# FRACTIONAL CUSTOMER ADAPTATION GUIDE

## Purpose
This guide helps you scale CIDO services for different customer sizes, time commitments, industries, and business needs. Use it to quickly configure engagement models and prioritize modules based on customer context.

---

## 1. CUSTOMER SIZE SCALING MATRIX

### Small Organizations (10-50 employees)

**Characteristics:**
- Limited IT staff (0-2 people)
- Basic technology infrastructure
- Minimal formal processes
- Budget constraints ($5K-25K/year for fractional CIDO)
- Need quick wins and hands-on guidance

**Module Prioritization:**
1. **Module 5: Cybersecurity & Risk** (CRITICAL - highest vulnerability)
2. **Module 15: Business Process Automation** (immediate ROI)
3. **Module 4: Cloud Infrastructure** (scalability foundation)
4. **Module 12: Financial Acumen** (budget optimization)
5. **Module 2: IT Transformation Strategy** (basic roadmap)

**Scaled Approach:**
- Focus on vendor-managed solutions vs. building internal capability
- Emphasize SaaS platforms over custom development
- Combine multiple modules into integrated solutions
- Provide implementation support, not just strategy
- Create simple 1-page documentation vs. formal governance

**Time Allocation (10-15 hours/month):**
- 40% hands-on implementation support
- 30% strategic guidance
- 20% vendor management
- 10% documentation

**Key Deliverables:**
- Essential security baseline (Module 5)
- Cloud migration to managed services (Module 4)
- 2-3 automation quick wins (Module 15)
- Basic IT budget with ROI tracking (Module 12)
- Simplified 90-day roadmap (Module 2)

**Time Allocation (15-25 hours/month):**

**Key Deliverables:**

## 5. ENGAGEMENT MODEL SELECTOR

### Decision Tree: Which Model Is Right?

**START HERE:**

**Question 1: What is the customer's primary need?**
- A) Quick wins and immediate ROI → Go to Question 2
- B) Strategic direction and planning → Go to Question 3
- C) Major transformation program → Go to Question 4
- D) Executive leadership gap → Go to Question 5

**Question 2: Quick Wins Path**
Customer urgency level?
- High urgency, limited budget → **5-10 hours/month** + Quick Win Stack (Modules 15+12+5)
- Moderate urgency, reasonable budget → **20 hours/month** + Quick Win Stack + Foundation Builder

**Question 3: Strategic Path**
Does internal team exist?
- Strong team, need direction → **5-10 hours/month** + Advisory model
- Weak/no team, need guidance → **10-20 hours/month** + Strategic + tactical hybrid

**Question 4: Transformation Path**
Organization size?
- Small (10-50) → **20 hours/month** + Quick Win + Foundation
- Medium (51-250) → **20-40 hours/month** + Foundation + specific domain stacks
- Large (251-1000) → **40+ hours/month** + Enterprise Transformation

**Question 5: Executive Gap Path**
Duration of gap?
- Temporary (3-6 months) → **40+ hours/month** + Fractional Executive
- Permanent solution → **40+ hours/month** + Fractional Executive + build internal successor

## 6. SCALING BEST PRACTICES

### Universal Scaling Principles

**1. Start Small, Prove Value, Expand**
- Begin with 1-2 modules maximum
- Deliver measurable results in 30-60 days
- Use success to justify expansion

**2. Match Engagement to Customer Maturity**
- Low maturity → More hands-on, more implementation
- High maturity → More strategic, less tactical

**3. Right-Size Documentation**
- Small customers: 1-page summaries
- Medium customers: Essential frameworks
- Large customers: Comprehensive governance

**4. Leverage Existing Frameworks**
- Don't reinvent what customers already have
- Build on existing processes and tools
- Integrate vs. replace where possible

**5. Focus on Business Outcomes, Not IT Activities**
- Always connect to revenue, cost, or risk
- Speak business language, not technical jargon
- Measure what matters to the business

### Common Adaptation Mistakes to Avoid

**❌ Don't:**
- Apply enterprise frameworks to small companies
- Over-document for tactical engagements
- Ignore industry-specific requirements
- Start with too many modules simultaneously
- Skip quick wins in favor of long-term strategy

**✅ Do:**
- Scale complexity to organization size
- Create fit-for-purpose deliverables
- Research industry compliance requirements
- Sequence modules for momentum
- Balance quick wins with strategic work

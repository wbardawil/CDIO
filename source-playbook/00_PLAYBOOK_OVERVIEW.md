# FRACTIONAL CIDO PLAYBOOK & ROADMAP SYSTEM
## Based on the CIDO Certificate Program

---

## 📋 SYSTEM OVERVIEW

This comprehensive playbook provides fractional Chief Information and Digital Officers (CIDOs) with a structured framework to deliver world-class digital transformation guidance to clients who cannot afford full-time executive leadership.

### What's Included

This system contains:

1. **Assessment Framework** - Diagnostic tools for evaluating digital maturity
2. **16 Modular Playbooks** - One for each CIDO competency area
3. **Roadmap Generator** - Templates for 30/60/90-day and 6/12-month plans
4. **Engagement Framework** - Meeting agendas, QBRs, and communication tools
5. **Value Demonstration Toolkit** - ROI calculators and success metrics
6. **Fractional Adaptation Guide** - Scaling guidance by client size and hours

## 🚀 QUICK START GUIDE

### For New Client Engagements

1. **Initial Assessment** (Week 1)
   - Run the Digital Maturity Assessment (see Section 1)
   - Identify top 3-5 priority modules
   - Document current state and pain points

2. **Strategic Planning** (Week 2)
   - Create customized 90-day roadmap
   - Prioritize quick wins vs. strategic initiatives
   - Set success metrics and milestones

3. **Execution** (Weeks 3-12)
   - Implement module-specific playbooks
   - Monthly check-ins and adjustments
   - Track progress against KPIs

4. **Review & Iterate** (Quarterly)
   - Conduct Quarterly Business Review
   - Demonstrate ROI and value delivered
   - Plan next quarter priorities

## 📊 FRACTIONAL TIME ALLOCATION GUIDE

### 5 Hours/Month (Starter)
- Focus: 1-2 modules maximum
- Best for: Advisory role, strategic guidance
- Typical modules: Strategy (#2), Financial Acumen (#12)

### 10 Hours/Month (Standard)
- Focus: 2-3 modules actively
- Best for: Strategic planning + tactical oversight
- Typical modules: Strategy (#2), Architecture (#3), Vendor Management (#13)

### 20 Hours/Month (Comprehensive)
- Focus: 3-5 modules actively
- Best for: Strategic + operational leadership
- Typical modules: Full transformation work across multiple competencies

### 40+ Hours/Month (Executive)
- Focus: Organization-wide transformation
- Best for: Interim CIDO role
- Typical modules: All 16 modules as enterprise initiatives

# ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK
## Digital Maturity Diagnostic System

---

## 🎯 ASSESSMENT OVERVIEW

This framework evaluates client maturity across all 16 CIDO competency areas using a 4-level scale:
- **Level 1: Beginner** - Ad hoc, reactive, minimal capability
- **Level 2: Developing** - Some processes, inconsistent execution
- **Level 3: Proficient** - Defined processes, reliable execution
- **Level 4: Advanced** - Optimized, innovative, industry-leading

---

## 📊 ASSESSMENT ADMINISTRATION

### Recommended Approach

**Initial Meeting (2 hours)**
- Review assessment with key stakeholders
- Complete high-level scoring for all 16 modules
- Identify top priority areas

**Deep Dive Sessions (1 hour each)**
- Focus on 3-5 highest priority modules
- Gather detailed evidence
- Document specific gaps and opportunities

**Results Presentation (1 hour)**
- Visual maturity scorecard
- Gap analysis
- Recommended prioritization
- Preliminary roadmap

---

## 🔍 MODULE ASSESSMENTS

### MODULE 1: Role of the CIDO

#### Diagnostic Questions

**1.1 Leadership & Governance**
□ Is there a clearly defined technology leadership role at the executive level?
□ Does the technology leader report directly to CEO or Board?
□ Are technology initiatives aligned with business strategy?
□ Is there a formal IT governance structure?

**1.2 Strategic Influence**
□ Does technology leadership participate in strategic business planning?
□ Is IT viewed as a strategic enabler vs. cost center?
□ Are there regular executive briefings on technology initiatives?
□ Does technology leadership influence product/service strategy?

**Maturity Scoring:**
- **Level 1**: No formal technology leadership role; IT is reactive and operational
- **Level 2**: Technology manager exists but limited strategic influence
- **Level 3**: Defined CIDO/CIO role with some board interaction and strategic input
- **Level 4**: CIDO is strategic partner, drives innovation, full board engagement

---

### MODULE 2: IT/Digital Transformation Strategy

#### Diagnostic Questions

**2.1 Strategy Development**

□ Does the strategy align with business objectives?

**2.2 Strategy Execution**

## 📊 ASSESSMENT SCORING & VISUALIZATION

### Scoring Methodology

For each module:
1. Answer diagnostic questions (Yes/No/Partial)
2. Review maturity level descriptions
3. Assign overall score: 1 (Beginner), 2 (Developing), 3 (Proficient), 4 (Advanced)
4. Document specific evidence and gaps

### Visual Spider/Radar Chart

Plot all 16 modules on a spider chart to visualize:
- Current state maturity
- Target state goals
- Gaps requiring attention

### Prioritization Matrix

Plot modules on 2x2 matrix:
- **X-axis**: Current Maturity (1-4)
- **Y-axis**: Business Impact (Low-High)

**Priority Quadrants:**
- **High Impact + Low Maturity** = TOP PRIORITY
- **High Impact + High Maturity** = Maintain/Optimize
- **Low Impact + Low Maturity** = Defer/Monitor
- **Low Impact + High Maturity** = Quick Wins/Best Practices

## 🎯 ASSESSMENT BEST PRACTICES

1. **Be Objective** - Use evidence-based scoring, not aspirational
2. **Involve Stakeholders** - Get input from business and technology leaders
3. **Document Everything** - Capture specific examples and evidence
4. **Focus on Gaps** - Understanding weaknesses drives improvement
5. **Prioritize Ruthlessly** - Not everything can be top priority
6. **Link to Business** - Connect maturity to business outcomes
7. **Set Baselines** - Reassess quarterly to track progress

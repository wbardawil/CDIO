## 💡 BEST PRACTICES SUMMARY

### Assessment Phase
✅ Start every engagement with assessment
✅ Involve business and technology stakeholders
✅ Document current state with evidence
✅ Prioritize based on business impact
✅ Set baseline metrics for value tracking

✅ Align technology initiatives with business priorities

✅ Get executive sponsorship before launching

## 📞 TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Problem: Low stakeholder engagement
**Solutions:**
- Revisit meeting schedules and format
- Demonstrate quick wins to build credibility
- Connect initiatives more clearly to business priorities
- Involve stakeholders in planning, not just status

### Problem: Difficulty demonstrating value
**Solutions:**
- Set up baseline metrics if not already done
- Focus on quick wins with clear ROI
- Use before/after comparisons
- Collect and share user testimonials
- Quantify soft benefits (productivity, risk reduction)

### Problem: Roadmap not progressing
**Solutions:**
- Review resource allocation and availability
- Identify and resolve blockers
- Reprioritize based on current constraints
- Break large initiatives into smaller chunks
- Escalate resource issues to sponsor

### Problem: Scope creep
**Solutions:**
- Document and track all requests
- Use decision framework for prioritization
- Show impact of additions on timeline/budget
- Propose phased approach
- Get executive sponsor involvement

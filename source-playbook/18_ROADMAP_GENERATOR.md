## 🎯 ROADMAP PHILOSOPHY

### Core Principles

**1. Balance Quick Wins with Strategic Impact**
- First 90 days: Demonstrate value with visible wins
- 6 months: Build momentum with foundational capabilities
- 12 months: Achieve transformational milestones

**2. Align with Business Priorities**
- Every roadmap item ties to business outcome
- Stakeholder buy-in on prioritization
- Clear ROI or strategic value

**3. Manage Risk and Dependencies**
- Sequence initiatives logically
- Address foundational capabilities first
- Build momentum progressively

**4. Stay Flexible**
- Quarterly reassessment and adjustment
- Respond to changing business needs
- Learn from early initiatives

## 📊 PRIORITIZATION FRAMEWORK

### Value vs. Effort Matrix

**Methodology:**
For each potential initiative, score:

**Value Score (1-10):**
- Business impact (revenue, cost, risk): 1-4 points
- Strategic alignment: 1-3 points
- Stakeholder priority: 1-3 points

**Effort Score (1-10):**
- Time/duration: 1-3 points
- Resource requirements: 1-3 points
- Technical complexity: 1-2 points
- Organizational change: 1-2 points

**Priority Calculation:**
- **High Priority**: High Value (7-10) + Low Effort (1-4)
- **Strategic Bets**: High Value (7-10) + High Effort (7-10)
- **Quick Wins**: Medium Value (4-6) + Low Effort (1-4)
- **Defer**: Low Value (1-3) or Low Value + High Effort

## 📅 30/60/90-DAY PLAN TEMPLATE

### Purpose
Establish credibility and momentum in first 3 months through quick wins, assessments, and relationship building.

### 30-Day Plan: "Learn & Assess"

**Week 1: Immersion**
□ Stakeholder interviews (CEO, CFO, business leaders)
□ Technology team meetings and 1-on-1s
□ Document review (strategies, budgets, architecture docs)
□ System and infrastructure tours
□ Quick assessment of critical risks
□ **Deliverable:** Initial observations memo

**Week 2: Deep Dive**
□ Complete digital maturity assessment
□ Analyze technology spending and contracts
□ Review active projects and initiatives
□ Identify immediate risks or opportunities
□ Begin vendor relationship review
□ **Deliverable:** Assessment findings presentation

**Week 3: Quick Win Identification**
□ Prioritize 2-3 quick wins (deliverable in 60-90 days)
□ Develop preliminary improvement recommendations
□ Socialize findings with key stakeholders
□ Identify governance or process gaps
□ **Deliverable:** 90-day plan proposal

**Week 4: Planning & Alignment**
□ Present 90-day plan to leadership
□ Refine based on feedback
□ Establish meeting cadences and governance
□ Begin first quick win initiatives
□ Set success metrics
□ **Deliverable:** Approved 90-day roadmap

**30-Day Success Metrics:**
- Stakeholder interviews completed: 100%
- Assessment delivered: Yes/No
- Quick wins identified: 2-3
- Governance established: Yes/No
- Executive approval: Yes/No

---

### 60-Day Plan: "Quick Wins & Foundation"

**Week 5-6: Execute Quick Wins**
□ Launch quick win initiative #1
□ Launch quick win initiative #2
□ Begin foundational work (process, governance, tools)
□ Conduct vendor review and negotiations
□ Develop strategic roadmap outline
□ **Deliverable:** Quick win #1 progress update

**Week 7-8: Build Momentum**
□ Complete quick win #1
□ Progress quick win #2
□ Establish financial management processes
□ Implement risk/issue tracking
□ Refine strategic recommendations
□ **Deliverable:** Quick win #1 results, benefits measured

**60-Day Success Metrics:**
- Quick win #1 completed: Yes/No
- Measurable value delivered: $XXX or [metric]
- Strategic roadmap drafted: Yes/No
- Stakeholder satisfaction: Score out of 5
- Governance functioning: Yes/No

---

### 90-Day Plan: "Strategy & Momentum"

**Week 9-10: Complete Quick Wins**
□ Complete quick win #2
□ Launch quick win #3 (if applicable)
□ Finalize 6-12 month strategic roadmap
□ Complete business cases for major initiatives
□ Establish quarterly business review process
□ **Deliverable:** Quick win #2 complete, strategic roadmap

**Week 11-12: Strategic Planning**
□ Present strategic roadmap to executive team
□ Secure approvals and budget allocation
□ Plan Year 1 strategic initiatives
□ Establish project governance
□ Conduct 90-day retrospective
□ **Deliverable:** Approved strategic roadmap, 90-day review

**90-Day Success Metrics:**
- Quick wins completed: 2-3
- Value delivered: $XXX or [metrics]
- Strategic roadmap approved: Yes/No
- Budget secured for initiatives: Yes/No
- Stakeholder Net Promoter Score: +XX

## 🗓️ 6-MONTH STRATEGIC ROADMAP TEMPLATE

### Structure

**Months 1-2: Foundation & Quick Wins** (Already covered in 30/60/90-day plan)
- Assessment and planning
- Governance establishment
- Quick wins delivered
- Strategic roadmap developed

**Months 3-4: Strategic Initiative Launch**

**Focus Areas (Select 2-3):**
□ **Technology Strategy**: Finalize and communicate digital transformation strategy
□ **Architecture Modernization**: Begin cloud migration or system modernization
□ **Security Enhancement**: Implement priority security controls or risk mitigation
□ **Cost Optimization**: Execute vendor optimization or cloud cost management
□ **Data & Analytics**: Launch data platform or BI initiative
□ **Team Development**: Restructure organization or upskill team

**Key Activities:**
- Launch 2-3 strategic initiatives
- Establish project governance and tracking
- Continue operational improvements
- Monthly progress reviews
- Vendor negotiations and contract renewals

**Months 5-6: Momentum & Optimization**

**Focus Areas:**
□ Progress strategic initiatives to 50-75% complete
□ Launch second wave initiatives
□ Optimize processes and governance based on learnings
□ Demonstrate ROI on completed work
□ Plan next phase (months 7-12)

**Key Activities:**
- Complete or significantly progress Q1 initiatives
- Conduct Quarterly Business Review
- Update strategic roadmap based on results
- Secure funding for next phase
- Celebrate successes and share learnings

## 📆 12-MONTH TRANSFORMATION ROADMAP TEMPLATE

### Annual Structure

**Q1 (Months 1-3): Foundation**
- **Theme:** "Assess, Plan, Quick Wins"
- **Focus:** Stabilize, gain trust, establish governance
- **Deliverables:** 
  - Complete assessment
  - Strategic roadmap approved
  - 2-4 quick wins delivered
  - Governance established
  - Budget secured

**Q2 (Months 4-6): Launch**
- **Theme:** "Strategic Initiative Execution"
- **Focus:** Launch major initiatives, build capabilities
- **Deliverables:**
  - 2-3 strategic initiatives launched
  - First major capability delivered
  - Cost optimization in progress
  - Team skills enhanced
  - QBR #1 completed

**Q3 (Months 7-9): Scale**
- **Theme:** "Expand and Accelerate"
- **Focus:** Scale successful initiatives, expand scope
- **Deliverables:**
  - Q2 initiatives 80%+ complete
  - New initiatives launched
  - Measurable business impact
  - Process optimization
  - QBR #2 completed

**Q4 (Months 10-12): Optimize**
- **Theme:** "Demonstrate Value and Plan Ahead"
- **Focus:** Complete initiatives, measure ROI, plan Year 2
- **Deliverables:**
  - Major initiatives complete
  - ROI documented
  - Year 2 strategy and budget
  - Transformation scorecard
  - Annual review

## 🚀 QUICK WIN IDENTIFICATION METHODOLOGY

### Quick Win Criteria

A good quick win:
✅ Deliverable in 30-90 days
✅ Requires minimal budget or resources
✅ Has visible business impact
✅ Builds credibility and trust
✅ Addresses known pain point
✅ Low organizational risk
✅ Provides learning for larger initiatives

### Quick Win Categories & Examples

**1. Cost Optimization**
- SaaS license consolidation (30-60 days, $XXk savings)
- Cloud cost optimization (30-45 days, 20-40% reduction)
- Vendor contract renegotiation (45-60 days, 10-20% savings)

**2. Process Improvement**
- IT request/ticket workflow optimization (30-45 days)
- Report automation (30-60 days)
- Approval process streamlining (30 days)

**3. Security Quick Wins**
- MFA implementation (30-45 days)
- Security awareness training (30 days)
- Vulnerability patching (ongoing, show 30-day progress)

**4. User Experience**
- Dashboard or portal improvement (45-60 days)
- Self-service capability (60-90 days)
- Mobile access to key system (60-90 days)

**5. Data & Visibility**
- Executive dashboard creation (30-45 days)
- Key metrics tracking (30 days)
- Budget visibility improvement (30-45 days)

## 📋 ROADMAP CUSTOMIZATION BY CLIENT SIZE

### Small Business (<100 employees)

**30/60/90-Day Focus:**
- Lighter assessment (1-2 weeks)
- 1-2 quick wins maximum
- Simple governance
- Focus on immediate operational improvements

**6-Month Focus:**
- 2-3 strategic initiatives maximum
- Heavy focus on cost optimization
- Cloud migration if relevant
- Basic security and compliance

**12-Month Transformation:**
- Foundational capabilities only
- 3-5 major initiatives total
- Emphasis on operational excellence
- Pragmatic technology choices

### Mid-Market (100-1000 employees)

**30/60/90-Day Focus:**
- Comprehensive assessment
- 2-3 quick wins
- Formal governance establishment
- Balance operations and strategy

**6-Month Focus:**
- 3-4 strategic initiatives
- Architecture modernization begins
- Security and risk management
- Analytics and BI foundation

**12-Month Transformation:**
- Comprehensive capabilities
- 6-8 major initiatives
- Full digital transformation program
- Innovation and emerging tech pilots

### Enterprise (1000+ employees)

**30/60/90-Day Focus:**
- Enterprise-scale assessment
- 3-4 quick wins showing diverse impact
- Complex governance structure
- Change management essential

**6-Month Focus:**
- 5-6 strategic initiatives
- Enterprise architecture program
- Advanced security posture
- Data and AI strategy execution

**12-Month Transformation:**
- Enterprise-wide transformation
- 10-15 major initiatives
- Scaled agile and DevOps
- Innovation labs and R&D

## ✅ ROADMAP SUCCESS CHECKLIST

A good roadmap:

□ Aligns with business priorities (not just technology priorities)
□ Includes quick wins in first 90 days
□ Balances run/grow/transform initiatives
□ Has clear success metrics for each initiative
□ Is achievable with available resources
□ Includes appropriate buffers and contingency
□ Has been reviewed and approved by stakeholders
□ Has defined governance and review cadence
□ Is flexible enough to adjust as needed
□ Communicates clearly to different audiences

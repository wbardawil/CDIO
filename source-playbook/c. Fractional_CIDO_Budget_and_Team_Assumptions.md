# Fractional CIDO Service Delivery: Budget and Team Assumptions

**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Detailed cost structure and staffing model for delivering fractional CIDO services  
**Based on:** Fractional CIDO Engagement Roadmap

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the complete cost structure, team composition, and resource assumptions required to deliver fractional CIDO services profitably across all three phases (Diagnostic, Quick Wins, Fractional Engagement) and all company sizes (Small, Mid-Size, Large Mid-Market).

**Key Financial Assumptions:**
- **Target Gross Margin:** 50-65% across all service lines
- **Utilization Target:** 70-80% for billable resources
- **Overhead Rate:** 25-35% of direct labor costs
- **Subcontractor Markup:** 30-40% when needed

## Phase 1: Diagnostic Assessment - Budget & Team

### Small Company Diagnostic ($5,000 - $10,000)

#### Team Composition & Time Allocation

**Lead Consultant (Fractional CIDO Principal)**
- **Profile:** 15+ years experience, former CIO/CTO, CIDO certified
- **Rate:** $250-$350/hour
- **Time Commitment:** 24-32 hours over 2 weeks
- **Activities:**
  - Executive stakeholder interviews (8 hours)
  - Assessment framework execution (8 hours)
  - Analysis and recommendations (6 hours)
  - Report writing and presentation prep (2-4 hours)
  - Client presentation (2 hours)

**Senior Analyst (Optional for larger scope)**
- **Profile:** 5-8 years experience, technical background
- **Rate:** $150-$200/hour
- **Time Commitment:** 0-16 hours
- **Activities:**
  - Data collection and documentation (8 hours)
  - Technical assessments (4 hours)
  - Report assistance (4 hours)

**Total Labor Hours:** 24-48 hours

#### Cost Structure

**Direct Costs:**
- Lead consultant: $6,000 - $11,200 (24-32 hrs @ $250-350/hr)
- Senior analyst (if used): $0 - $3,200 (0-16 hrs @ $150-200/hr)
- **Total Direct Labor:** $6,000 - $14,400

**Indirect Costs:**
- Tools & software (assessment platform, reporting): $200 - $500
- Travel (if required, 1 day on-site): $0 - $800
- Administrative support (proposal, contracts, invoicing): $100 - $200
- **Total Indirect:** $300 - $1,500

**Total Cost:** $6,300 - $15,900

**Pricing:** $5,000 - $10,000  
**Gross Margin:** -26% to 37% (Lower margin acceptable as loss leader for Phases 2 & 3)

**Strategic Note:** Small company diagnostics often break even or run at a loss but serve as client acquisition with 70%+ conversion to Phase 2.

## Critical Success Factors & Risk Mitigation

### Key Assumptions

   - Senior resources: 60-70% billable
   - Mid-level resources: 70-80% billable
   - Risk: Client concentration, project gaps
   - Mitigation: Diversified client base (10+ active clients)

2. **Pricing Discipline:**
   - Maintain minimum margins: 40% on core services
   - Risk: Price pressure, scope creep
   - Mitigation: Value-based pricing, clear SOWs, change orders

3. **Contractor Management:**
   - Reliable network of 10-15 trusted contractors
   - Risk: Availability, quality, rate inflation
   - Mitigation: Multi-source, ongoing cultivation, framework agreements

4. **Client Conversion:**
   - 70% diagnostic → quick win conversion
   - 80% quick win → fractional engagement conversion
   - Risk: Budget constraints, competition
   - Mitigation: Strong ROI demonstration, flexible engagement models

5. **Retention Rates:**
   - Small companies: 12-month average engagement
   - Mid-size: 18-24 month average engagement
   - Large: 24-36 month average engagement
   - Risk: Client graduates to full-time CIDO, budget cuts
   - Mitigation: Continuous value delivery, regular business reviews

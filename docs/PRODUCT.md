# AI-CDIO: Fractional Executive Operating System

## What It Is

**The operating system for fractional Chief Digital & Information Officers and internal IT leaders.** It turns a 30-file CDIO playbook into interactive, client-aware engines that produce real deliverables — assessments, roadmaps, QBR decks, status reports, board memos — in minutes instead of hours.

Built first as a tool the founder uses on his own fractional practice. Validated in parallel with other practitioners and IT directors.

## The Promise

You stop starting from scratch. Every client engagement reuses 80% of your methodology. Every monthly status, every quarterly review, every board memo is generated from real client data and edited (not written) by you.

## Who It's For

| Segment | Job They're Doing | Core Value |
|---------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Fractional CDIO/CTO** (primary, founder-led) | Manages 3-8 client engagements, produces recurring deliverables, bills hourly or retainer | Save 5-10 hrs/month per client. Scale beyond the human ceiling. |
| **Internal IT Director / VP IT** (secondary, larger TAM) | Owns 1 company, presents to board, justifies budget, plans annual roadmap | Look strategic. Borrow CDIO frameworks. Grow toward CIO. |
| **Aspiring fractional CDIOs** (pipeline) | IT directors who plan to go fractional in 1-3 years | Career growth → eventual upgrade to practitioner tier |

## In Scope (What We DO)

| Capability | What It Means |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Practitioner Workspace** | Multi-client portfolio for fractionals. Single-org for directors. Same data model, different UI. |
| **Assessment Engine** | 5-level maturity scoring across 16 modules. Multi-stakeholder, divergence detection, AI-powered Decision Packages. |
| **Roadmap Engine** | 30/60/90-day, 6-month, 12-month plans. Value/Effort prioritization. Industry adaptation. |
| **Engagement Manager** | Monthly status reports, QBR decks, board memos, project charters. Generated from client data + AI narrative. |

| **Templates Library** | Vendor evaluations, M&A tech DD, risk registers, governance frameworks, stakeholder comms matrices. |
| **Knowledge Reuse** | "I solved this at Client A" → suggested for Client B. Cross-client learning per practitioner. |
| **Co-branded Client Portal** (Phase 2) | Each practitioner shares a branded view with their clients. |

## Out of Scope (What We DON'T Do)

| Boundary | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Implementation services | We advise + deliver artifacts. We don't configure systems. |
| Sell vendor products for commission | Our value is objectivity. Never become a sales channel. |
| Replace MSP infrastructure management | We complement MSPs, not compete on tickets/ops. |

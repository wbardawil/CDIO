# AI-CDIO: Product Definition

## The Promise

**AI-CDIO helps you make smart technology decisions for your business — without needing a CIO, without vendor bias, and without the politics.**

We help you understand where you are, what to do next, and track whether it's working.

## Target Market

- **Primary:** SMBs with 10-1,000 employees that have no dedicated CIO/CTO
- **Secondary:** Holdings, family offices, PE/VC funds managing multiple portfolio companies
- **Channel:** MSPs, IT consultants, accountants who serve SMBs

## North Star Metric

**1,000,000 SMBs impacted worldwide.**

## In Scope (What We DO)

| Category | What AI-CDIO Does |
|----------|-------------------|
| **Assess** | Score technology maturity across 16 domains. Show where you stand vs similar companies. |
| **Prioritize** | Tell you what matters most for YOUR business — weighted by size, industry, and pain points. |
| **Recommend** | Specific, vendor-agnostic action cards: what to do, how, cost, and expected impact. |
| **Align** | Surface where leadership disagrees (divergence detection) and present objective data to resolve it. |
| **Track** | Monitor progress on actions and initiatives. Show ROI over time. Re-assess and adjust. |
| **Advise** | Answer technology questions in plain language. Triage issues. Explain tradeoffs. |
| **Report** | Generate assessments, roadmaps, progress reports for leadership and board presentations. |

## Out of Scope (What We DON'T Do)

| What | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Implement** (configure systems, write code, install software) | We advise, you execute. Keeps us vendor-agnostic. |
| **Sell products** (recommend vendors for commission) | Our value is objectivity. We're not an MSP. |
| **Manage infrastructure** (monitor servers, helpdesk) | MSPs do this. We complement them, not compete. |
| **Legal/compliance certification** | We flag risks, professionals certify. |
| **Replace human judgment** | We present data and recommendations. Humans decide. |
| **Guarantee outcomes** | We project based on industry data. Execution determines results. |

## The Three Entry Paths

### 1. Chat-First (Primary)
"What's your biggest technology challenge?" → 3-5 conversational questions → immediate assessment + action card. Value in 5 minutes, no signup.

### 2. Pain-Point Selection
Grouped chips: Problems (systems, security, spending), Aspirational (AI, no IT team), Discovery (don't know what I don't know, full health check).

### 3. Full Assessment
16-module structured assessment with diagnostic questions. Multi-stakeholder support. Spider chart, priority matrix, divergence detection, 90-day roadmap.

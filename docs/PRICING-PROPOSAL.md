## TL;DR — one page

**Five tiers + one services overlay.** Each tier respects the three locked architectural laws (methodology FULL on every tier; scale-only differentiation; Mechanism 1/2 split for AI compute). Tier 3 is where both buyer motions converge.

| # | Tier | Persona | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Free Quick Scan | Anyone — lead capture | $0 (watermarked, 1 module) |
| 1 | **Professional** | IT Manager, IT pro, junior IT Director | **$49-79/mo** ($490-790/yr) |
| 2 | **Team / Department** | IT Director running a 3-15 person team | **$299-499/mo flat or $50-75/seat/mo** |
| 3 | **Executive / Full IT Function** | Full-time CIO, IT Director at no-CIO co, operational CTO | **$1.5-2.5K/mo or $18-30K/yr** |
| 4 | **Fractional Practice** | Independent fractional CDIO/CIO/CTO | **$500-1500/mo per practitioner + per-client fee** |
| 5 | **CEO Engagement** (services overlay) | Mid-market CEO buying 90-day engagement | **$15-30K per 90-day** + Tier 3 subscription after |

**Core principle:** *price the OUTCOME a persona can credibly claim, anchored to what their alternative costs — not the seats or the modules.* Same product, wildly different prices justified by wildly different outcomes per persona. That's the geometry that makes scale-based tiering work without violating "methodology FULL on every tier."

**Phased rollout:** Tier 5 only today → add Tier 0 + 1 when Quick Scan ships → add Tier 2 with Phase 1.5/1D → add Tier 3 + 4 with Phase 2.5 commercial release → lock final numbers Phase 3.

## ⚖️ Founder decisions needed at this review

1. **Tier 5 (CEO Engagement) band — $15-30K per 90-day.** Pick the number you're going to quote on the next outbound. My recommendation: **$20K** as the publishable price, with a structured upside to $30K for larger / more complex engagements (>$50M revenue companies, >2 simultaneous initiatives). $20K = 1/5th of full-time CIO at $400K — the math is clean for outbound copy.

2. **Tier 1 (Professional) — $49 vs $79?** I'd publish **$59/mo** (or annual at $590/yr — round, memorable, sits between Pluralsight ($45) and Notion+ ($96 for plus, $20 for Pro)). Validates against the IT Manager self-expensable band ($100/mo ceiling).

3. **Free Quick Scan — gated or open?** I'd gate behind email + work info (LinkedIn-style). Generates qualified leads + funds the AI compute. Open Quick Scan = pure cost center with no flywheel.

## Per-tier rationale (the "why these numbers" briefs)

### Tier 1 / Professional — the IT Manager unlock

- **$49-79/mo is the self-expensable band** — buyable on personal credit card, no boss approval. Above $100/mo → budget conversation needed.
- **Not free** for the individual: paid signals commitment; free Quick Scan handles lead-gen separately.
- **Watermarked output** is the upsell mechanism: full methodology + Coach Mode, but exports say *"Personal Assessment — not an official organizational deliverable."* Protects Tier 2 upsell + handles the ethics question of an IT Manager generating a "board memo" without org sign-off.
- **Coach Mode is the retention hook:** even if the current role doesn't need it, they're learning toward the next role. Value is in the user, not the role → churn-resistant.

### Tier 2 / Team — the bottom-up wedge

- IT Director discretionary budget: typically $5-20K/yr. $3-6K/yr fits without C-level approval.
- **The most important tier for the product motion** — converts Tier 1 individuals: an IT Manager using it personally for 3 months, then telling their IT Director boss *"I want our whole team on this."* Notion/Linear/Figma playbook.
- Offer both pricing models: flat ($299-499/mo) for ≤8 seats; per-seat ($50-75/user) above. Default flat for small teams.

### Tier 3 / Executive — the in-house CIO motion

- **$18-30K/yr matches mid-market GRC pricing** (Vanta/Drata/Hyperproof comp-set you actually fight for budget against).
- CIO discretionary threshold typically $25-50K — buyable without board approval.
- Buyer math: *"$25K to avoid one $200K consulting engagement on AI strategy."*
- **White-label outputs are critical here** (the CIO wants the board memo on their company's letterhead, not yours).

### Tier 4 / Fractional Practice — home turf

- A fractional billing $200-300/hour can justify $1K/mo platform spend that lets them serve 2-3 more clients.
- **Per-client fee** is honest but **capped (≤$200-400/mo per active client)** so the math always works for the practitioner. No perverse incentive to avoid onboarding.
- BYOK essential at this tier — AI compute scales linearly with client count.

### Tier 5 / CEO Engagement — the high-ticket services anchor

- The 90-day commitment matrix monetized as a SKU.
- **$15-30K positioned at 1/5th of a full-time CIO annual cost** — directly steals budget from the "we should hire a CIO" conversation.
- Post-engagement Tier 3 subscription handles recurring revenue. **Engagement = wedge; subscription = moat.**
- Only exists because the founder is a practicing fractional. If founder stops selling engagements, becomes a referral product for certified fractionals (Tier 4 revenue).

## Risks + what to validate before Day 37-38 final lock

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 cannibalises Tier 2 (10 individuals at $49 vs Team at $499 = same outcome) | Watermarked exports + single-user-only Coach Mode + no multi-stakeholder assessments on Tier 1 |
| Tier 4 fractional pricing hard to land — practitioners price-sensitive | Validate with 3-5 friendly fractionals in Year 2+. Alternative model: "first 3 clients free" runway, or % of practitioner revenue (high-trust, needs reporting) |
| CEO Engagement at $15-30K feels "consulting" not "platform" | Frame as: *"$X for the 90-day engagement; the platform comes with it. Continued access from $1.5K/mo."* Separates services from subscription cleanly. |
| AI cost on Tier 1-2 unpredictable | Tight allowance modeling from Day 19+ telemetry. Start conservative on allowance; relax as data accumulates. |
| Free Quick Scan = cost center? | Limit to 1 per email/quarter; require email + work info. Lead value funds the cost. |
| Priced higher than Vanta/Drata low-tiers (different product, same comparison happens) | Explicit positioning: *"We are the strategy + governance + AI layer ABOVE compliance tools. Vanta tells you if you're compliant. We tell you what to do next, in the language your board reads."* |

**Three things to validate before locking final numbers:**

1. **Real cost telemetry** (existing locked decision) — per-engagement AI compute cost; informs Tier 1-2 allowance + Tier 3+ BYOK margin
2. **Tier 5 → Tier 3 conversion rate** — does the CEO who buys a 90-day engagement actually subscribe after? If <50%, the model breaks
3. **Tier 1 → Tier 2 upgrade rate** — does the individual IT Manager actually pull their team onto Team tier? If not, individual is a low-margin distraction

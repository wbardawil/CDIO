# AI-CDIO: Risks, Blind Spots & Open Questions

## Johari Window Applied to AI-CDIO

|  | **Known to YOU** | **Unknown to YOU** |
|---|---|---|
| **Known to OTHERS** | **OPEN / ARENA** | **BLIND** |
| **Unknown to OTHERS** | **HIDDEN / FACADE** | **UNKNOWN** |

### OPEN — Don't compete here
- Fractional executive market is exploding (2K → 110K LinkedIn fractionals in 2 years)
- AI can generate decent strategic content
- 16-module CDIO methodology exists publicly (CMU base)
- ScalePad, Fortium, Shiny operate in adjacent space
- Internal IT directors lack tools and want to look strategic

**Implication:** No competitive advantage in the obvious facts. Don't waste pitches on "explaining the market."

### HIDDEN — Your unfair advantage (lean into it)
- Your specific 30-file synthesis (vs CMU's 18 + your annotations)
- Real fractional engagement experience — judgment calls not in any document
- Patterns across YOUR clients — which problems repeat, which interventions work
- The integration of assessment → roadmap → deliverable as ONE flow
- Your specific client wins/losses
- Your reputation in the fractional community

**Implication:** Build network effects on this. Eventually open-source the playbook (creates trust + viral distribution). Keep your patterns + workflow + judgment as the proprietary moat.

### BLIND — Fill via discovery calls (Week 1-2)
- What internal IT directors actually do all day
- What buyers actually compare you to in their head
- Why fractionals would NOT switch from spreadsheets/Notion
- Procurement processes inside SMBs
- ScalePad's actual 2026 roadmap
- What other fractional CDIOs find embarrassing (= what they'd quietly pay to avoid)
- Career anxiety driving 40% of director buying decisions
- Why some fractional CDIOs charge $10K/mo and others $80K

**Implication:** 30 discovery calls in Week 1-2 = your highest-ROI investment. Cost: $0. Information value: huge.

### UNKNOWN — Existential, can't engineer for
- Whether AI replaces fractional CDIOs entirely in 3-5 years
- Whether the methodology is obsolete by 2028
- Whether next AI model makes assessment + roadmap trivial
- Whether SMBs have IT directors at all in 5 years

**Implication:** Make the platform adaptable. Don't optimize for any specific assumption about AI capability. Stay close to customer pain.

## MECE Risk Categories

### 1. MOAT RISK — "Why won't they build it themselves?"

| Risk | Mitigation | Who Owns |
|------|------------|----------|
| Tech-savvy CIOs replicate via Claude prompts | Workflow lock-in (templates, decision history, action cards) — copying prompts ≠ copying workflow | Product |
| Customer extracts data after 6 months | Network effects: anonymized benchmarks, shared templates, community | Product |
| "Vendor agnostic" is table stakes, not moat | Real moats are workflow, data network effects, brand authority | Strategy |
| ScalePad pivots to attack solo fractionals | Speed: ship faster than they can pivot. Specificity: stay focused on solo practitioners they won't go after | Speed |

**The blind spot:** You've assumed "playbook + AI" is the moat. The real moats are workflow lock-in, data network effects, and community. None are built by default.

### 2. MARKET RISK — "Is the demand durable?"

| Risk | Mitigation | Who Owns |
|------|------------|----------|
| AI-native companies (post-2024) skip fractional CDIOs entirely | Track cohort: do post-2024 SMBs hire fractionals at same rate? | GTM |
| Fractional growth is recession-driven (laid-off execs), not secular | Talk to 2018 fractional hirers — are they still using? | GTM |
| Platform itself becomes the "fractional CDIO" replacement | Reframe as feature, not bug — but means buyer changes | Strategy |
| LinkedIn supply (110K fractionals) is supply-side fad | Validate demand: companies hiring fractionals, not just calling themselves fractional | GTM |

### 3. EXECUTION RISK — "Can you actually deliver?"

| Risk | Mitigation | Who Owns |
|------|------------|----------|
| Your fractional clients need you when platform breaks | Time-boxed founder commitment, on-call rotation if growing team | Founder |
| 5 paying customers call simultaneously with bugs | Customer support model defined upfront (email-only initially, response SLA 24h) | Operations |
| Cannot have 1 day/week without firefighting | Automate ruthlessly. Background jobs, observability, automated alerts. | Engineering |
| Product depends on you noticing what's broken | Build in user-facing error reporting. Telemetry on broken paths. | Engineering |

**The blind spot:** Founder-product fit can become founder-trap. Customer #0 = good. Customer #1-100 forever depending on founder = bad.

### 4. POSITIONING RISK — "Are you in the right slot?"

| Risk | Mitigation | Who Owns |
|------|------------|----------|
| Should be CERTIFICATION + COMMUNITY, not SaaS | Validate: does your audience pay for tools or for credentials? Run ONE "AI-CDIO Methodology" workshop, see who shows up | Strategy |
| Should be ACQUIRED by ScalePad, not compete | Build to compete OR build to be acquired = different products. Decide by Month 6. | Strategy |
| Should be OPEN SOURCE + paid services | Linux model: free engine, paid expertise. 10x distribution. Risk: monetization harder. | Strategy |
| Should be info-product, not software | The playbook IS the product. Software is delivery. Different pricing, different markets. | Strategy |

**The blind spot:** "SaaS" is one of many monetization models. Validate the CHOICE before locking in.

### 5. PERSONAL/STRUCTURAL RISK — "Are you set up to win?"

| Risk | Mitigation | Who Owns |
|------|------------|----------|
| Existing fractional clients see this as competing | Direct conversation with each. Frame as: "I'm building tools to serve YOU better." | Founder |
| Fractional revenue drops while building | 30% reduction stress test in financial model. Only build if you can survive 6 months at 70% revenue. | Founder |
| Family tolerance for two jobs (consultant + founder) | Set explicit time boundaries. Communicate runway honestly. | Founder |
| Capital raise pressures away from moat | If you raise, get terms that protect IP. Or don't raise. | Founder |
| Conflict: fractional CDIO competitor signs up | Decide: serve all (max revenue) or restrict (protect own clients)? Document policy. | Founder |

**The blind spot:** Most founder failures are domestic, not technical. Personal sustainability constrains everything.

## The 7 Things to Do Before Production (Pre-Launch Checklist)

1. **Talk to 30 prospects in 14 days.** Fill the BLIND quadrant. Not to validate the product — to learn what you don't know.

2. **Run a "build vs buy" stress test on yourself.** Pretend you're a tech-savvy IT director. Try to recreate AI-CDIO's value with Claude + Notion + a Kanban board. Where does it break? That's your real moat. If it doesn't break, you don't have a product.

3. **Talk to 3 of your current fractional clients about your build.** Are they a customer (want it for their team) or a threat (feel you're competing with their CDIO)? Know in Week 1, not Month 12.

4. **Define your network-effects hypothesis explicitly.** Write: "By customer #1,000, switching out costs them ___ because ___." If you can't fill it in, design features that change the answer.

5. **Decide your relationship with ScalePad NOW.** Compete, complement, partner, or be acquired. Each path requires different early decisions.

6. **Set a 90-day kill switch.** "If by Day 90 I don't have ___, I stop building." Without it, sunk-cost fallacy kills founders.

7. **Write down what you'd open-source vs keep proprietary.** Hidden quadrant (synthesis, judgment, patterns) stays. Playbook content might be more powerful as open source.

| Low conversion (<1% from discovery calls) | Medium | High | Day 14 of validation sprint |
| ScalePad pivots to attack | Low | High | Watch their roadmap announcements |

| User data breach | Low | Very High | Pre-launch security review |
| Anthropic API price hike or deprecation | Medium | High | API announcements |
| Founder burnout / fractional clients suffer | High | High | Personal energy management |
| Regulatory changes (AI governance) | Medium | Medium | Watch EU AI Act updates |

The first three are the ones to actively defend against. The last five are watchful waiting.

# AI-CDIO: Outcome Log

**Purpose:** the founder's weekly verification surface that the platform delivers what `STRATEGY-2026.md` promises **to his CEO clients**. Year 1 audience is the CEO via the founder; this log captures what the founder DELIVERED to those CEOs each week.

**Discipline:** every Friday, the founder writes one entry answering: *"What did the platform help me deliver to my CEO client this week that I couldn't have done as well without it?"*

**This log is THE Year 1 evidence base.** Day 90 kill switch reads this log. Paying-customer count from other fractionals is a Year 2 metric. Year 1 metric is **CEO outcomes delivered** — measured here in specific dollar amounts saved, specific decisions caught, specific board moments won, alignment created, real AI rollouts shipped.

**Day 90 review criteria (revised 2026-05-07 evening):**
- Has the founder delivered all six 90-Day Commitment Matrix milestones (`docs/STRATEGY-2026.md`) to at least <anchor client>?
- Are there 5+ specific CEO outcomes logged below?
- Is the founder capacity-constrained on his fractional practice (the trigger for Year 2 commercial release)?

If yes to all three → Phase 3 commercial release (open to other fractionals). If no → keep dogfooding; the platform's job is to make the founder a better fractional CDIO before it's a business.

**Format for each entry (CEO-led, locked 2026-05-07 evening):**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD (Week N — Phase X Day Y)

**Client:** <CEO's company name — e.g., "<anchor client>">
**CEO outcome delivered:** <one-sentence describing what the CEO experienced or now has>
**90-Day Commitment Matrix milestone:** <which Day 14 / 21 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 milestone this hits, or "between milestones">
**Pillar served:** <Project Success | Tech ROI | Strategy Alignment | Moat-Building | Other>
**Detail:** <2-4 sentences. Specific dollar amounts, specific decisions caught, specific feelings the CEO expressed.>
**Counterfactual:** <what would have happened without the platform — e.g., "would have taken 2 more weeks; CEO would have funded the wrong vendor; board would have asked for justification we didn't have">
**Better / cheaper / faster:** <how this outcome compares to what a fractional WITHOUT AI-CDIO, a Big 4 consulting firm, or a full-time CDIO would have delivered>
```

**Tagging guide for "Pillar served":**
- **Project Success** — caught a misalignment / killed a doomed initiative / lifted ship rate
- **Tech ROI** — surfaced over-spend / killed a bad investment / focused budget on high-leverage moves
- **Strategy Alignment** — CEO and team on same page / monthly cadence visible / board-ready artifact
- **Moat-Building** — proprietary data / AI use case with structural advantage / defensibility lift
- **Other** — practitioner-side wins (time freed, learning loop, retention)

**The "Better / cheaper / faster" field is required.** It forces the founder to articulate what the platform actually changed about the engagement quality — which is exactly what the Differentiated Promise depends on. Without it, the outcome log devolves into feature-usage anecdotes.
